2. What data we process and why

We only process the data you voluntarily give us and the data strictly needed for the Website to work, plus, if you consent, statistical browsing data. Each processing activity is described below with its purpose, legal basis and retention period.

2.1. Contact form

  • Data: name, email address, company or brand (optional), the service you are interested in and your message.
  • Purpose: to answer your enquiry and, if you ask for one, prepare a quote.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the box accepting this policy (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: if the enquiry does not lead to a project, the data is deleted 12 months after the last contact. If it does, section 2.3 applies.

2.2. Email and WhatsApp messages

  • Data: whatever you give us when you write (name, email address or phone number and the content of your messages).
  • Purpose: to reply and continue the conversation you started.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and, where applicable, pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: as in section 2.1.

2.3. Clients

  • Data: identification, contact and billing details needed to provide the contracted service.
  • Purpose: to manage the project, invoicing and the client relationship.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and compliance with legal, accounting and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Retention: for the duration of the relationship and afterwards for the legally required periods (for example, six years for accounting records under article 30 of the Spanish Commercial Code).

2.4. Browsing the Website

  • Data: IP address and technical connection data (browser and device type, date and time of access).
  • Purpose: to serve the pages and ensure the technical operation and security of the Website.
  • Legal basis: the controller’s legitimate interest in the proper functioning and security of the Website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: technical logs are kept by the hosting provider for the limited period set out in its policy; the controller does not use them for any other purpose.

2.5. Web analytics (Google Analytics)

  • Data: the identifier assigned by the analytics cookie, pages visited, where the visit came from (including campaign parameters in links and QR codes), device and browser type, approximate location (country and city, without storing the IP address) and clicks on contact links.
  • Purpose: to obtain aggregated statistics on how the Website is used and how well its campaigns work, in order to improve it. They are not used to identify you or for advertising.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by clicking "Accept" in the cookie notice (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and article 22.2 LSSI-CE). You can withdraw it at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.
  • Retention: user-level data is kept in Google Analytics for 2 months; after that only aggregated statistical reports remain. The cookies expire after 13 months (see the Cookie policy).

The fields marked as required in the form are needed to answer your enquiry; without them we cannot reply. All other data is optional. Accepting analytics cookies is always voluntary and does not affect your use of the Website.

6. Your rights

Data protection law gives you the following rights, which you can exercise free of charge:

  • Access: to know whether your data is being processed and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification: to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Erasure: to have your data deleted when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.
  • Restriction: to have processing suspended in the cases provided by law.
  • Portability: to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller.
  • Objection: to object to processing on grounds relating to your particular situation.
  • Withdrawal of consent: to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

To exercise any of these rights, write to hellovincastudio@gmail.com with the subject "Data protection", or to the postal address in section 1, stating which right you wish to exercise and enclosing a copy of a document proving your identity. We will reply within one month at most.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid; www.aepd.es) if you believe your data has not been processed lawfully.

7. Security

The controller has adopted appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, in accordance with Article 32 GDPR. Among them, the Website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and only the controller has access to the messages received.

8. Source of the data

The data comes only from you, provided voluntarily through the Website's form, the published contact channels or, if you accept them, the analytics cookies. We do not obtain data from public sources or from third parties.

9. Automated decisions and profiling

No automated decisions producing legal effects are taken and no profiling within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR is carried out.

10. Commercial communications

We do not send commercial communications or newsletters without your prior express consent, in accordance with article 21 LSSI-CE.

11. Minors

The Website's services are not aimed at children under fourteen. Under article 7 LOPDGDD, processing the data of a child under fourteen requires the consent of their parents or guardians. If we find that a child's data has been collected without that consent, it will be deleted immediately.

12. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in law, case law or the way the service is provided. Users will be clearly informed on the Website of any substantial changes.
